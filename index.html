Working title · Roblox

Anime Tower Defense

Collect units, build teams, and fight through stages and challenges. A high quality anime tower defense game with distinctive systems, exciting combat, and long term progression.

01The game at a glance

Director Jeffee123’s goal: distinctive systems, exciting combat, and presentation that stands out. Detailed models, smooth animation, impressive VFX, and polished UI.

CollectingSummoning units across 7 rarities, with rare pulls treated as big cinematic moments.
StrategyPlacement, team composition, upgrades and abilities that reward thinking, not just placing units and waiting.
Long term progressionTraits, evolutions, limit breaks, raids, tournaments and events that keep players working toward something.

02Core gameplay

  • Place units around maps to defend against enemy waves
  • Upgrade units during matches
  • Use different unit abilities and attacks
  • Build teams around different strategies
  • Fight stronger enemies and bosses as they progress
  • Earn rewards to improve their teams and unlock more content

Combat must be fun and strategic. Where a unit is placed and which units a player brings should matter.

03Units and rarities

Collecting units is a core part of the game. Every unit must feel unique, with its own design, attacks, abilities and playstyle. Units must never feel like copies of each other with different appearances.

Common Rare Epic Legendary Mythic Secret ORIGIN

Mythic units are also the fuel for Limit Breaks. ORIGIN is a special top rarity with exclusive units and events only. Secret units are hidden and very rare pulls.

04Summoning system

Summoning must be exciting. Getting a really rare unit should feel like a big moment, not just a character appearing on screen.

  • Summoning Center in the lobby
  • Different unit rarities with different summon chances
  • Cinematic summoning animations, with special animations for rare pulls
  • Limited time summon events
  • Exclusive units connected to certain events

05Unit combat and placement

One of the biggest systems in the game. Units have different attack styles, abilities and roles. Some focus on damage, others have different uses depending on the situation.

  • Unit placement, enemy paths and waves
  • Targeting systems
  • Damage, range and attack speed stats
  • Unique attacks and abilities, plus passive abilities
  • Boss fights, different enemy types and difficulty levels

06Upgrades, Limit Break, Traits, Evolution

  • In-match upgrades: units can be upgraded during matches with earned currency. Upgrades must make a noticeable difference to damage, range, attack speed, or how a unit’s attacks work.
  • Limit Break: sacrifice 5 Mythic units to raise a unit’s maximum level by 10. Handled at the Limit Break Center. Extra Mythic pulls stay valuable, and Limit Breaks become a major long term goal.
  • Traits: customize units and give them additional benefits. Another layer of progression and another reason to keep improving favourite units.
  • Evolution: certain units evolve into stronger versions, with improved stats and possibly new appearances, attacks or abilities. Handled at the Evolution Center.

09Trading system

  • Players trade units through the Trading Plaza
  • Trading supports the collecting loop and player interaction
  • Security is a hard requirement: the system must be built to prevent exploits and duping

10Maps, enemies, bosses, story

  • No two maps should feel the same. Different environments per world, unique enemy designs per world
  • Bosses must feel like their own encounters, not bigger normal enemies
  • Each world has its own atmosphere and challenges
  • Long term progression: collecting rarer units, upgrades, traits, evolutions, Limit Breaks, harder stages, raids, tournaments, special events

Story: an original storyline with mysteries revealed gradually. Players discover things naturally instead of having everything explained up front.

  • ZERO: an original unit created by Jeffee123, important to the story
  • ORIGIN: the special rarity is tied to the game’s bigger mysteries

11Visual and quality bar

  • Detailed anime-inspired unit models
  • Smooth animations and unique attack animations
  • Impressive ability VFX and cinematic summoning animations
  • Detailed maps and environments, polished UI
  • Unique boss designs and effects, special visual effects for rare units and important events
