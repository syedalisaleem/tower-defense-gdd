01

The Game at a Glance

What we're building, in one place.

A high-quality anime tower defense game on Roblox. Players collect units, build teams, and fight through stages and challenges. The game stands on three pillars:

Collecting

Summoning units across 7 rarities, with rare pulls treated as big cinematic moments.

Strategy

Placement, team composition, upgrades and abilities that reward thinking — not just placing units and waiting.

Long-term progression

Traits, evolutions, limit breaks, raids, tournaments and events that keep players working toward something.

Director's goal: distinctive systems, exciting combat, and presentation that stands out — detailed models, smooth animation, impressive VFX, polished UI. This document is the single source of truth for the game's vision, written from Game Director Jeffee123's plan so every team member works from the same page.

02

Core Gameplay

Fun and strategic — placement and team choice must matter.

Players will be able to:

  • Place units around maps to defend against enemy waves
  • Upgrade units during matches
  • Use different unit abilities and attacks
  • Build teams around different strategies
  • Fight stronger enemies and bosses as they progress
  • Earn rewards to improve their teams and unlock more content

Combat must be fun and strategic. Where a unit is placed and which units a player brings should matter.

03

Units and Rarities

Every unit feels unique — never a copy with a different appearance.

Collecting units is a core part of the game. Every unit must feel unique — its own design, attacks, abilities and playstyle. Units must never feel like copies of each other with different appearances.

04

Summoning System

A rare pull should feel like a big moment.

Design rule

Summoning must be exciting. Getting a really rare unit should feel like a big moment, not just a character appearing on screen.

  • Summoning Center in the lobby
  • Different unit rarities with different summon chances
  • Cinematic summoning animations
  • Special animations for rare pulls
  • Limited-time summon events
  • Exclusive units connected to certain events
05

Unit Combat and Placement

One of the biggest systems in the game.

Units have different attack styles, abilities and roles — some focus on damage, others have different uses depending on the situation.

Systems needed:

  • Unit placement
  • Enemy paths and waves
  • Targeting systems
  • Damage, range and attack speed stats
  • Unique attacks and abilities
  • Passive abilities
  • Boss fights
  • Different enemy types and difficulty levels
06

In-Match Upgrades

Units can be upgraded during matches with earned currency. Upgrades must make a noticeable difference: increased damage, range or attack speed, or changes to how a unit's attacks work.

07

Limit Break System

Push units beyond their normal cap.

Effect
+10 Max Level

A Limit Break increases a unit's maximum level by 10.

Cost
5 Mythic Units

To perform a Limit Break, players must sacrifice 5 Mythic units.

  • Purpose: extra Mythic pulls stay valuable, and Limit Breaks become a major long-term progression goal
  • The Limit Break Center in the lobby handles this system
08

Trait System

Traits let players customize their units and give them additional benefits — another layer of progression and another reason to keep improving favourite units.

09

Evolution System

  • Certain units can evolve into stronger versions
  • Evolved units get improved stats and potentially new appearances, attacks or abilities
  • Handled at the Evolution Center in the lobby

12

Trading System

  • Players trade units with each other through the Trading Plaza
  • Trading supports the collecting loop and player interaction
Hard requirement

Security: the trading system must be built to prevent exploits and duping.

13

Maps, Enemies and Bosses

  • No two maps should feel the same — different environments per world
  • Unique enemy designs per world
  • Bosses must feel like their own encounters, not bigger normal enemies
  • Each world has its own atmosphere and challenges
14

Long-Term Progression

Players should always have something to work toward.

  • Collecting new units and rarer units
  • Upgrading units
  • Unlocking traits
  • Evolving units
  • Using Limit Breaks
  • Completing harder stages
  • Raids and tournaments
  • Special events
15

Story and Lore

Mysteries revealed gradually — discovered, not explained up front.

ZERO

An original unit created by Jeffee123, important to the story.

The special rarity is tied to the game's bigger mysteries.

Key story elements — details are the Director's, revealed over time. The game has an original storyline with mysteries revealed gradually; players discover things naturally instead of having everything explained up front.

Open Decisions

Not yet decided — needs the Director's call. No numbers have been invented for these.

How to read this list

These are not in the Director's plan yet and need his call before or during the build. If you're a new member and you're wondering about one of these — it's undecided, not missing.

  • Summon chances per rarity, and summon currency (earned, premium, or both)
  • Launch scope: how many units, maps and Story worlds in version 1
  • Unit roles list (damage, support, crowd control, economy, etc.)
  • Trait list and how traits are rolled / changed
  • Evolution requirements (items, currency, level)
  • Enemy and boss roster per world
  • Monetization: gamepasses and dev products, if any
  • Trading rules: which rarities can be traded, trade limits, anti-scam measures
  • The game's final name

Maintained as development progresses. Changes to the vision come from the Game Director; system implementation details are added by the developer who owns that system.
