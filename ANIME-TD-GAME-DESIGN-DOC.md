# Anime Tower Defense — Game Design Doc

*Working title. This document is the single source of truth for the game's vision, written from Game Director Jeffee123's plan so every team member works from the same page. Last updated: Nov 27, 2026.*

## 1. The Game at a Glance

A high-quality anime tower defense game on Roblox. Players collect units, build teams, and fight through stages and challenges. The game stands on three pillars:

- **Collecting** — summoning units across 7 rarities, with rare pulls treated as big cinematic moments
- **Strategy** — placement, team composition, upgrades and abilities that reward thinking, not just placing units and waiting
- **Long-term progression** — traits, evolutions, limit breaks, raids, tournaments and events that keep players working toward something

**Director's goal:** distinctive systems, exciting combat, and presentation that stands out — detailed models, smooth animation, impressive VFX, polished UI.

## 2. Core Gameplay

Players will be able to:

- Place units around maps to defend against enemy waves
- Upgrade units during matches
- Use different unit abilities and attacks
- Build teams around different strategies
- Fight stronger enemies and bosses as they progress
- Earn rewards to improve their teams and unlock more content

Combat must be fun and strategic. Where a unit is placed and which units a player brings should matter.

## 3. Units and Rarities

Collecting units is a core part of the game. Every unit must feel unique — its own design, attacks, abilities and playstyle. Units must never feel like copies of each other with different appearances.

## 4. Summoning System

Summoning must be exciting. Getting a really rare unit should feel like a big moment, not just a character appearing on screen.

- **Summoning Center** in the lobby
- Different unit rarities with different summon chances
- Cinematic summoning animations
- Special animations for rare pulls
- Limited-time summon events
- Exclusive units connected to certain events

## 5. Unit Combat and Placement

One of the biggest systems in the game. Units have different attack styles, abilities and roles — some focus on damage, others have different uses depending on the situation.

Systems needed:

- Unit placement
- Enemy paths and waves
- Targeting systems
- Damage, range and attack speed stats
- Unique attacks and abilities
- Passive abilities
- Boss fights
- Different enemy types and difficulty levels

## 6. In-Match Upgrades

Units can be upgraded during matches with earned currency. Upgrades must make a noticeable difference: increased damage, range or attack speed, or changes to how a unit's attacks work.

## 7. Limit Break System

- A Limit Break increases a unit's maximum level by **10**
- Cost: sacrifice **5 Mythic units**
- Purpose: extra Mythic pulls stay valuable, and Limit Breaks become a major long-term progression goal
- The **Limit Break Center** in the lobby handles this system

## 8. Trait System

Traits let players customize their units and give them additional benefits — another layer of progression and another reason to keep improving favourite units.

## 9. Evolution System

- Certain units can evolve into stronger versions
- Evolved units get improved stats and potentially new appearances, attacks or abilities
- Handled at the **Evolution Center** in the lobby

## 10. Game Modes

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| Story Mode | Stages, worlds, enemies and bosses across the game's storyline |
| Raids | Harder encounters with powerful bosses and better rewards |
| Tournaments | Competitive play — players compete and compare team performance |
| Challenges | Extra tests of strategy and team composition |

Each mode must give players something different to work toward.

## 11. Lobby and Main Hub

The lobby is a real explorable environment, not a basic menu.

| Location | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Summoning Center | Summon new units |
| Evolution Center | Evolve units |
| Limit Break Center | Limit Break units |
| Trading Plaza | Trade units with other players |
| Raid Gate | Enter raids |
| World Portals | Travel to Story Mode worlds |

Planned social features in the lobby and game: party system, multiplayer gameplay, trading, competitive leaderboards, and social interaction in the lobby.

## 12. Trading System

- Players trade units with each other through the Trading Plaza
- Trading supports the collecting loop and player interaction
- **Security is a hard requirement:** the system must be built to prevent exploits and duping

## 13. Maps, Enemies and Bosses

- No two maps should feel the same — different environments per world
- Unique enemy designs per world
- Bosses must feel like their own encounters, not bigger normal enemies
- Each world has its own atmosphere and challenges

## 14. Long-Term Progression

Players should always have something to work toward:

- Collecting new units and rarer units
- Upgrading units
- Unlocking traits
- Evolving units
- Using Limit Breaks
- Completing harder stages
- Raids and tournaments
- Special events

## 15. Story and Lore

The game has an original storyline with mysteries revealed gradually — players discover things naturally instead of having everything explained up front.

Key story elements (details are the Director's, revealed over time):

- **ZERO** — an original unit created by Jeffee123, important to the story
- **ORIGIN** — the special rarity is tied to the game's bigger mysteries

## 16. Visual and Quality Bar

- Detailed anime-inspired unit models
- Smooth animations and unique attack animations
- Impressive ability VFX
- Cinematic summoning animations
- Detailed maps and environments
- Polished UI
- Unique boss designs and effects
- Special visual effects for rare units and important events
